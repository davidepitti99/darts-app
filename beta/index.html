Fit confidence

The colored line is the worse of two scores:

Low — weak precision or model mismatch
Fair — usable, still wide ±% or soft KS
Good — solid n / CI and acceptable model fit
High — large n, tight CI, strong model fit

1. σ precision — how tightly σ is known from sample size n
Low: n < 8
Fair: n < 40 or ±% wider than ~16%
Good: n < 100 or ±% wider than ~8%
High: n ≥ 100 and a tight ±% band

2. Model fit (KS p) — do distances look like a Rayleigh curve at this σ?
KS p < 0.01 → mismatch (caps at Low)
KS p < 0.05 → weak (caps at Fair)
KS p < 0.20 → acceptable
KS p ≥ 0.20 → good model fit

±% and (lo–hi mm) are the 95% CI on σ. KS p is how surprising the curve mismatch would be if the model were true.

Accuracy σ

σ (mm) is your typical dart scatter — how far throws land from the aim on average. Lower σ means a tighter thrower; optimal aims change with it.

Estimate yours in Fit σ from bull practice, then apply it to a seat.
